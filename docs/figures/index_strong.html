Genes with strong evidence of a different 3' end between BSF and PCF

A gene is here when, among the reads assigned to it, at least one end cluster changes its share by 30 points or more between stages, every replicate of one stage lies above every replicate of the other, the same cluster changes in the same direction under both other cluster settings, and the cluster holds 20 or more assigned reads; the gene and the cluster pass stageR at a nominal gene-level overall false discovery rate of 5 percent (docs/methods.md, section 7). A read is assigned to the gene when at least 50 of its aligned bases lie inside the gene's CDS, its mapping quality is 20 or more, no secondary alignment of it scores at least as high as the primary, and it covers no other CDS on the same strand. The rule resolves the read's origin as far as the alignments allow; it is not an experimental proof of origin. Usage is the share of the gene's assigned ends. The window change is the same cluster's change in the window analysis, whose denominator is every end in the window. In the zoomed figure, reads of uncertain origin under this rule (no CDS overlap, low mapping quality, equal placement elsewhere, another CDS covered) are grey. Every figure reads 5' to 3' from left to right; for minus-strand genes the x axis is reversed and the axis label says so.
